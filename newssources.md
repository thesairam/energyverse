# 🌐 9. Google News RSS (VERY IMPORTANT)

Use this to massively expand coverage across **all countries + languages**

### Examples:

Solar:
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=solar+energy

Wind:
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=wind+energy

Battery:
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=battery+storage

EV:
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=electric+vehicles

V2G:
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=vehicle+to+grid

👉 You can also localize:

```
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=solar+energy&hl=en-NL&gl=NL&ceid=NL:en
```
